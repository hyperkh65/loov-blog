President Lee Jae-myung stated that Ukraine's official announcement on the 24th regarding the return of two North Korean prisoners of war to South Korea was a deeply regrettable decision that broke a confidential agreement. Currently on a state visit to Mexico, the President strongly criticized the move via his social media, calling it irresponsible to reveal such matters to the world while disregarding national security and the safety of the individuals involved. This diplomatic conflict came to the surface after Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy directly mentioned the return in his speech at the UN General Assembly the previous day.

date: 2026-09-25 08:37:28 +0900

The Korea Exchange (KRX) conducted a meaningful volunteer activity to deliver the warmth of sharing directly to marginalized seniors in the Busan region, celebrating Chuseok, Korea's traditional harvest festival. This sharing event went beyond simple financial aid; employees personally packed gift baskets and visited each household to check on their well-being, adding deeper significance to the initiative. Designed to provide practical support to neighbors who struggle to feel the festive atmosphere, this activity has received widespread acclaim both within and outside the local community. With rising prices and an economic downturn increasing the number of vulnerable groups struggling to prepare for the holiday, such good deeds by public institutions are truly welcome news.

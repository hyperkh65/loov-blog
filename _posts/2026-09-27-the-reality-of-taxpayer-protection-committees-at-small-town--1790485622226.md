The Taxpayer Protection Committees at so-called second-tier tax offices located in small towns and suburban areas across the country are suffering from severe operational stagnation, with meetings often not held even once a year. This situation urgently calls for institutional improvements. For self-employed individuals and small and medium-sized business owners living in the regions, the channel through which they should seek redress for unfair tax assessments and protect their rights is effectively shut down.

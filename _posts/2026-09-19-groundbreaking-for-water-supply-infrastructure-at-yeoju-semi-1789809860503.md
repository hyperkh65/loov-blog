The Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment announced that construction has officially begun on the priority section of Phase 1 of the Integrated Water Supply Project for the Yeoju Semiconductor Industrial Complex on September 18. This prompt commencement was made possible by the approval and official announcement of the project implementation plan on the same day. Semiconductor manufacturing processes do not tolerate even a one-second interruption and require a constant, massive supply of water. In response to these industrial demands, the government and relevant agencies have rolled up their sleeves to ensure that companies' factory construction schedules are not compromised.

Joongheung Group has announced that it will pay approximately 70 billion won in construction fees ahead of schedule to support the smooth capital management and operational stability of its partners in anticipation of the upcoming Chuseok holiday. This initiative is a joint effort by Joongheung Construction and Joongheung Land to assist small and medium-sized partners who often face financial difficulties due to concentrated capital demands during holidays. Notably, this payment is being made entirely in cash rather than through commercial bills, which is expected to provide substantial relief to partners.

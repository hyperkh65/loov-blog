The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) announced on the 17th (local time) a sudden innovation exemption plan to temporarily allow tokenized stocks—where physical assets are converted into digital form for trading—for the next five years. This measure, which takes immediate effect, is an extraordinary step taken just two days after the Clarity Act, aimed at comprehensively supervising the virtual asset market, was rejected in the Senate. This move opens the door for employees and students, who were previously restricted to trading only during New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) opening hours, to freely buy and sell stocks day and night.

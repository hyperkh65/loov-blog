The book value of the disposed plant was only about 3.2 billion won, but the actual transaction amount reached 15 billion won, drawing significant attention from the market. Notably, the sale gain alone accounts for nearly 40 percent of the company's total market capitalization, an enormous scale. It is significant that the company has monetized a non-core asset that was previously used for cast steel product production lines but had been dormant for a long period.

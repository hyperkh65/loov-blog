Woo Sang-hyuk won the bronze medal in the men's high jump final at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games, clearing a height of 2.22m on September 27, 2026, at the Mizho Park Athletics Stadium in Nagoya, Japan. This achievement marks a remarkable feat, as he has now stood on the Asian Games podium for three consecutive Games, following his silver medals at the 2018 Jakarta-Palembang and 2023 Hangzhou editions. The competition took place in rainy, unfavorable weather conditions that were physically demanding for the athletes. Despite these challenges, his characteristic bright smile and composed approach to the competition resonated deeply with many spectators and viewers.

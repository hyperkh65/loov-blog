Choi Hui-young, Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism, successfully concluded a meeting with the Mexican Minister of Culture to foster close cultural cooperation between the two nations. This gathering was arranged to achieve tangible results in various fields, including pop culture and the tourism industry, based on the long-standing friendship between the two countries. It is truly welcome news for us, who have long been eager for cultural exchanges with the South American region. In particular, with the global K-wave trend, interest in Korean pop culture in Mexico is soaring. Amidst this momentum, the government is actively stepping up to play a pivotal bridging role between the two countries.

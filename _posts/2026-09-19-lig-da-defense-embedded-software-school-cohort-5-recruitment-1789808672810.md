LIG D&A has partnered with the Korea Radio Promotion Association (KRPA) to recruit trainees for the 5th cohort of the Defense Embedded Software School, a job-linked training program designed to cultivate core practical talent in the defense industry. This 5th cohort marks the final iteration of the program and is expected to offer the highest level of quality and benefits to date. It presents a prime opportunity for job seekers aspiring to become embedded software development experts who design the "brains" of advanced weapon systems such as missiles and aircraft. A total of 30 elite candidates will be selected, with applications accepted until 9:00 AM on the 28th.

The government and local authorities are conducting comprehensive safety inspections of housing facilities for families affected by major wildfires in the Gyeongsangbuk-do region ahead of the upcoming holiday. This meticulous care aims to help residents who lost their homes in the recent disaster spend the approaching Chuseok holiday with a bit more warmth and comfort. As the autumn breeze turns chilly, it has become essential to carefully check the heating functions and potential leaks in the temporary prefabricated houses. Through these measures, various strategies are being implemented to reduce daily inconveniences for displaced residents and promote practical housing stability.

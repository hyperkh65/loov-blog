Park Wan-soo, the Governor of Gyeongnam Province, personally visited Tongyeong and Geoje, areas that suffered significant damage from record-breaking heavy rains this summer, to inspect holiday prices and listen to residents' voices. This on-site visit went beyond a simple holiday greeting; it was arranged to console local merchants, firefighters, and self-reliance program participants who are overcoming the flood damage. It holds great significance in that the Governor directly checked the actual cost of living felt by citizens and sought immediate solutions on the ground. In a time when everyone is saying they are afraid to open their wallets ahead of Chuseok, the Governor's presence in traditional markets communicating with merchants provided a small comfort to many.

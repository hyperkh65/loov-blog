On October 1, 2026, a meeting of the Emergency Economic Headquarters, concurrently serving as the Economic Ministers' Meeting, was held at the Government Sejong Center in Seoul, chaired by Lee Hyeong-il, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance and Economy. At this meeting, a field-focused support plan for corporate investment and innovation, jointly prepared by relevant government ministries, was swiftly announced. The plan reflects a strong determination to restore economic vitality by incorporating on-site feedback and driving substantial investment execution.

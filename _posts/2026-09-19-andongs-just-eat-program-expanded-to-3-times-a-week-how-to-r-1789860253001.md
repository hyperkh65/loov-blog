Andong City has announced that it will significantly expand its "Just Eat" program from twice a week to three times a week starting this month. This initiative is designed to help residents who are facing sudden livelihood crises and are struggling to secure their next meal. The most distinctive feature of this program is that it requires no income or asset verification documents, making it accessible to those who desperately need welfare benefits but have been unable to receive help due to complex bureaucratic procedures. Neighbors with empty refrigerators can immediately receive food and essential daily necessities worth approximately 20,000 won after going through a simple verification process.

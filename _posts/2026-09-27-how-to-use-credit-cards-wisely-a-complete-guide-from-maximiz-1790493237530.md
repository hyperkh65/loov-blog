Recently, many people are carefully comparing individual credit sales performance requirements and airline mileage accumulation benefits across different card companies to find the product that suits them best. However, using cards indiscriminately can easily lead to mistakes such as losing money on annual fees or incurring unnecessary fixed costs. In particular, there are hidden costs that are easy to overlook, such as fees incurred when canceling cards or paying taxes. In this article, we will explore specific tips for utilizing and managing credit cards more wisely.

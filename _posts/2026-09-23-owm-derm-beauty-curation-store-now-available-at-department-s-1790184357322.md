Optima Wellness Museum (OWM) has finally launched its first offline store in a department store, expanding its presence beyond the online realm. Located in a dedicated section of Code, a derma beauty concept store on the first basement floor of Hyundai Department Store Pangyo, which opened on the 18th, the store has already attracted significant attention. Previously, OWM provided beauty product curation and information exclusively through online channels, but now customers can experience the products firsthand in a physical space. This is wonderful news for those who have always felt limited by having to choose cosmetics based solely on on-screen descriptions.

Single-person households can receive up to 1.75 million KRW, while three-person households can receive up to 3.3 million KRW in substantial support. The Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC) program, operated by the National Tax Service, provides up to 1.15 million KRW (for the first half) or more, paid twice a year, by applying both labor deductions and income deductions to ensure economic stability for low-income earners. Applications for the first half of 2026 are due by September 15, while second-half applications are accepted until the deadline in December. You can apply easily via HomeTax or the mobile app without complex paperwork, ensuring no one misses out on these benefits. If you miss the confirmation period, you may forfeit benefits worth millions of KRW, so check now.

The suspect in the horrific freezer murder case that occurred at a cafe in Paju, Gyeonggi Province, a man in his 30s who owned the cafe, has been transferred to the prosecution under strict non-public conditions. This incident, in which a woman in her 60s, whom the suspect did not know at all, was locked in a sub-zero freezer and left to die, has sent shockwaves through the public. The suspect boarded a police transport vehicle with complete media blackout and was moved to the Goyang District Office of the Uijeongbu District Prosecutors' Office. Investigative authorities are currently focusing on uncovering the suspect's involvement in a gift certificate fraud scheme related to debts exceeding 1 billion won, as well as determining whether any accomplices were involved.

A grocery shopping application that allows consumers to compare prices and discount information for agricultural and livestock products at nearby stores at a glance has finally entered its pilot operation phase. This is very welcome news for housewives who have always had to physically visit multiple locations to compare prices every time they go shopping. This technology, jointly introduced by the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs and the Ministry of Science and ICT, is expected to make our daily lives significantly more convenient. In fact, it is now easy to find out which supermarket offers the best price for a bag of onions on the way home from work using just a smartphone.

The most advantageous choice regarding how to receive your retirement pension is to transfer it to an annuity account and receive it in installments to maximize tax benefits. If you are an employee approaching retirement, you are likely deeply concerned about how to manage the lump sum that will eventually land in your account. While receiving your severance pay all at once upon leaving a company may provide immediate financial security, it can result in a hefty tax bill. In fact, many retirees regret receiving their pension as a lump sum because they failed to calculate the tax implications properly. In this article, we will carefully examine the wisest ways to receive your retirement pension, tips for saving on taxes, and the actual application procedures.

If you choose to receive your retirement pension as a lump sum, you may have to pay taxes ranging from several million to tens of millions of won all at once, making it essential to consider receiving it as an annuity. Manager Kim, who retired at the mandatory retirement age, wanted to receive his accumulated retirement benefits as a large sum of cash immediately. However, he was greatly shocked when he received a tax notice for a much larger amount than he had expected. He later realized that if he had transferred this money to an Individual Retirement Pension (IRP) account and received it in monthly installments after the age of 55, he could have significantly reduced his tax burden.

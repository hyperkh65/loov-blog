The Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with the International Energy Agency (IEA) in Seoul on the 17th to strengthen climate crisis response and energy security in the Asian region. This collaboration is an innovative step aimed at overcoming two massive crises simultaneously: energy supply instability originating from the Middle East and global climate disasters. In fact, as tensions in the Middle East escalate, Asian countries with high fossil fuel dependencies are exposed to the risk of supply chain disruptions that could erupt at any moment.

News has emerged that Samsung Electronics is facing disruptions in its new product schedule due to a direct hit from a critical component supply shortage. Samsung has decided to postpone approximately 10% of this month's component production plan for the Galaxy Z Fold8, which it prepared ambitiously this year, to next month. This is because the supply of Display Driver Integrated Circuits (DDIs), which are essential for driving the screen, is not going smoothly. This measure directly reflects the reality of the component industry struggling to keep up with market reactions and demand that have proven hotter than expected.

The Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment has officially launched the 2026 Central Contract Market for Energy Storage Systems (ESS), beginning the recruitment of power generation businesses with a total capacity of 1,180MW across the mainland and Jeju. These facilities, which store energy during periods of surplus power generation and release it when needed, play a pivotal role in ensuring the stable operation of the power grid. Having already secured significant capacity through the previous three market rounds, the government aims to secure a large additional volume based on the 11th Basic Plan for Power Supply and Demand.

The Industry-Academia Cooperation Division and the Anchor Project Team of Seoul National University of Science and Technology (SNUST) have officially launched a joint development project for advanced defense component technologies in partnership with Korea Aerospace Industries (KAI), a leading aerospace company representing South Korea. This collaboration marks a significant turning point, going beyond simple research support to directly apply the university's superior engineering research capabilities to the real-world defense industry. In particular, the project aims to completely revolutionize the manufacturing of aircraft parts, where high-mix, low-volume production is essential, by combining artificial intelligence (AI) with manufacturing processes.

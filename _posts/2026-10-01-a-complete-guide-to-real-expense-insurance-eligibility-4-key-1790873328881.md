Contrary to the common belief that "everyone can enroll unconditionally," there are specific restrictions regarding eligibility for real-expense insurance (실비보험). Depending on your health check-up results, your premiums may increase, or you may be denied coverage for certain riders. Therefore, it is essential to accurately compare your current health status with the policy terms before enrolling. In particular, as of 2026, product structures have become more complex, with issues such as long-term hospitalization riders and the inclusion of optional non-covered medical expenses. Enrolling based on simple comparisons carries a high risk of future claim denials. Please review the following criteria to assess your situation.

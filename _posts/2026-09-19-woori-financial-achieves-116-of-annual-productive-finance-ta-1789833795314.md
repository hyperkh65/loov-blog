Woori Financial Group has achieved a remarkable milestone by deploying a total of 25.3 trillion won in the productive finance sector through August of this year, reaching 116% of its annual target ahead of schedule. This achievement was officially announced at the Advanced Strategic Industry Finance Council chaired by Chairman Lim Jong-ryong, emphasizing a focus on boosting real economic vitality beyond mere numerical targets. This result combines 1.5 trillion won in investments and 23.8 trillion won in loans, already reaching 35.7% of the five-year target for the Future Co-Growth Project. Building on its accumulated capabilities, Woori Financial plans to broaden its support scope to local regions and technology-driven small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).

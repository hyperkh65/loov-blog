The Korea Export-Import Bank (KEXIM) has decided to release a substantial fund of 5 trillion won in collaboration with Chungcheongbuk-do to drive regional balanced growth. Recently, KEXIM President Hwang Ki-yeon and Chungcheongbuk-do Governor Shin Yong-han met at the Chungcheongbuk-do Provincial Office in Cheongju to sign a comprehensive cooperation agreement aimed at activating the growth engine of the Chungcheong region and supporting large-scale projects. This agreement is set to serve as a catalyst for diffusing growth momentum, which has been concentrated in the capital region, to the provinces and securing a leading competitive edge in cutting-edge industries.

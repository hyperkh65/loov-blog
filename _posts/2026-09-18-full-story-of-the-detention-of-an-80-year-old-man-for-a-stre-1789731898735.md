An 80-year-old man has been detained by a court after wielding a knife against a 70-year-old man he knew, causing injuries on a street in Jeonnong-dong, Dongdaemun-gu, Seoul. Judge Kang Young-hoon, the presiding judge for arrest warrants at the Seoul Northern District Court, issued an arrest warrant for the 80-year-old suspect, identified as Mr. Lee, on suspicion of attempted murder. The decision was made following a pre-arrest interrogation on the 15th, citing concerns that the suspect might flee. The incident occurred around 8:20 PM on the 13th on a street in a busy or residential area of Jeonnong-dong.

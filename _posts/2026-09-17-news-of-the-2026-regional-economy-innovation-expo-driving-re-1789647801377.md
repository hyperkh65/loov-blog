This event is a meaningful gathering where over 200 diverse institutions and local governments from across the country come together to freely share outstanding cases of regional economic revitalization. It is expected to play a crucial role as a catalyst, bringing warmth to local commercial districts and small business owners who are struggling with recent high inflation and an economic downturn. On-site, visitors can witness and learn from live ideas that have actually revitalized local economies, going beyond mere exhibitions.

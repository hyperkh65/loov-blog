September consumer prices rose 2.9% year-on-year, but without the price stabilization efforts of the Joint Response Team, inflation could have surged to 3.5%. The Ministry of Finance and Economics held a Price-Related Ministries Meeting and a Government-wide Joint Response Team on Livelihood Infringements, chaired by Lee Joo-seop, Director of the Livelihood Economy Bureau. At the meeting, the oil price cap, discount support for agricultural, livestock, and aquatic products, and oil price stabilization policies were disclosed. Simultaneously, enforcement results against illegal private finance and ticket scalping were announced, demonstrating a strong will to prevent livelihood infringements.

During the upcoming Chuseok holiday, from September 24 to 27, EV charging fees will be significantly reduced by approximately 50% between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM. This measure has been implemented because overall electricity consumption decreases as factories and businesses close during the holiday, while power generation from sources like solar energy remains abundant. In a situation where surplus electricity would otherwise be wasted, encouraging EV charging helps stabilize the power grid while providing tangible economic benefits to drivers. Recalling the emergency power supply management measures taken during previous holidays, this initiative serves as an excellent strategy to protect both the environment and drivers' wallets.

Dukseung Women's University successfully held the "2026-2nd Semester Army General Campus Recruiting for Reserve Officers" event on September 8 and 9, at the Main Lecture Hall Building and the Student Union Building. The event attracted significant interest, with broad participation from current students facing job placement, graduating students, unemployed graduates, and local youth. It was organized to align with the expectations of young people seeking stable and specialized career paths in the military sector amidst the rapidly changing job market. A key differentiator from other recruitment fairs was the direct visit to the university campus by generals who actually lead military organizations, in partnership with the Army Personnel Command.

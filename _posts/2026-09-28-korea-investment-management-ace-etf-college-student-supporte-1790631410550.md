Korea Investment Management recently partnered with the ACE ETF College Student Supporters to successfully complete offline promotional events at Ewha Womans University and Dongguk University. The events aimed to raise awareness about Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs) and sound investment principles. This initiative drew significant attention because the student ambassadors, who have been actively engaged since late July, took the lead in every aspect, from planning to on-site operations. By communicating the importance of financial literacy and asset management at a level relatable to their peers, the students attracted substantial participation.

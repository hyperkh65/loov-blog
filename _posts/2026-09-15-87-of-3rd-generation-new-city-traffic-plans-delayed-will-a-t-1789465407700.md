It has been revealed that 87% of the schedules for key metropolitan traffic projects in the 3rd-generation new cities are facing delays, just as the first move-ins in Incheon Gyeoyang are scheduled for December this year. The government's long-standing principle of "transportation first, housing later," which was repeatedly emphasized whenever new cities were announced in the past, is once again on the verge of becoming nothing more than an empty slogan. According to data from the Korea Land & Housing Corporation (LH), the completion dates for 77 out of 89 total projects have been pushed back from the original plans, with a significant portion expected to be completed only after 2030.

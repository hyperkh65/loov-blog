Receiving your severance pay in an IRP account significantly reduces your tax burden. An IRP (Individual Retirement Pension) is a personal retirement pension that allows you to consolidate your severance pay upon job change or retirement to prepare for your own future. Manager Ahn emphasized that you must receive your severance pay in an IRP account to achieve tax savings. Since IRPs allow you to fill the annual tax deduction limit of 9 million KRW, your year-end tax refund amount also increases. Furthermore, utilizing an IRP when converting from a Defined Benefit (DB) plan to a Defined Contribution (DC) plan can also reduce the burden of severance income tax.

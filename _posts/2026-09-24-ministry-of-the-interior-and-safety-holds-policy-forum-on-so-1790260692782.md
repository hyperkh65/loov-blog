The Ministry of the Interior and Safety (MIS) held a policy forum to actively incorporate social values into government policies across the board, aiming to overcome the various national challenges and complex crises that have recently intensified. This event was organized with the purpose of firmly rooting public interest values—such as symbiosis with local communities, environmental protection, and respect for human rights—within the administrative framework, moving beyond the traditional approach that pursued only economic growth. The numerous disasters and social conflicts we experience in our daily lives have reached a level where they can no longer be resolved by piecemeal prescriptions, and consequently, the role of the government is being called for a fundamental change.

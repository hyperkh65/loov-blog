title: "News on Nonghyup's Additional 500 Million Won Support for Flood-Displaced Residents in Gyeongnam and Disaster Recovery Measures"
date: 2026-09-28 13:06:39 +0900
---

Nonghyup announced on the 22nd that it has provided an additional 500 million won in donations to support flood-displaced residents in the Gyeongnam region, particularly in Geoje and Tongyeong, which suffered severe damage from the heavy rains in August. Including this latest contribution, the total accumulated donations have reached 730 million won, offering comfort to the hearts of local residents. Notably, this initiative goes beyond a one-time donation; it was launched to comprehensively support the recovery of daily life and the resumption of agricultural activities for displaced residents ahead of the upcoming holiday season.

Gwangbokgu, Seoul, has finally completely shut down two restaurants that had been operating illegally without registration in the Inchecheon area of Wui-dong Valley. This measure has drawn significant attention as it targets establishments that had continued their brazen operations for over 20 years while evading enforcement. These businesses were notorious cases that persisted in their operations despite being subject to multiple criminal complaints. The district office posted notices at the entrances of the businesses informing the public of their illegal status and affixed seal stickers to cooking equipment and refrigerators.

This can be seen as an extraordinary measure to ease the financial burden on local residents, as recent price surges have significantly increased the cost of holiday preparations. Residents in certain areas, such as Hampyeong County in South Jeolla Province and Yeongcheon County in South Gyeongsang Province, have already received the 500,000 won in the form of prepaid cards and are using them wisely for grocery shopping and at traditional markets.

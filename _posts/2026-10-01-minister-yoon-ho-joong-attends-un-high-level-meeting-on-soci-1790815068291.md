We have received the good news that Yoon Ho-joong, Minister of the Ministry of the Interior and Safety, attended the UN High-Level Meeting on the Social Solidarity Economy held in New York, USA, to widely publicize South Korea's advanced policy directions and sign memoranda of understanding (MOUs) for close cooperation with Spain and Senegal. This event was a forum to seek solutions for reducing social disparities faced by the world and achieving sustainable growth. In particular, South Korea, being the first country in Asia to enact the Basic Act on Social Solidarity Economy, received significant attention at this meeting.

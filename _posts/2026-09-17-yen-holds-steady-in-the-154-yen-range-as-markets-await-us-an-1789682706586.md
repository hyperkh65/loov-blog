title: "Yen Holds Steady in the 154 Yen Range as Markets Await US and Japan Interest Rate Decisions"
date: 2026-09-17 22:05:06 +0900

On the 15th, the yen exchange rate in the Seoul foreign exchange market opened flat in the low 154 yen range per dollar, as a wait-and-see mood prevailed ahead of monetary policy decisions by the US Federal Reserve and the Bank of Japan. The primary factors driving this caution were soaring US bond yields and a spike in international oil prices due to deteriorating tensions in the Middle East, which heightened inflation concerns. Market participants are holding their breath to see what decisions the central banks of both nations will make. In particular, concerns over exchange rate volatility are deepening, given the Japanese economy's heavy reliance on energy imports. Rather than rushing to buy or sell, investors are focusing on monitoring upcoming economic indicators and policy direction...

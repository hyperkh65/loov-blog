As the appeal verdict for Mayor Oh Se-hoon approaches, attention is turning to the future of the Seun 4 District development plan. The first instance court sentenced him to a fine of 10 million won and a forfeiture of 21 million won, but the outcome of the appeal could determine whether he retains his position as mayor. A change in the mayor could potentially disrupt the high-rise development of the Seun 4 District, which has been led by the current administration. Therefore, it is important to examine both the progress of the trial and the key issues in the development plan.

Toss Bank shared a warm gesture by providing food supplies worth a total of 30 million won to children and guardians in multicultural families to celebrate the Chuseok holiday. This initiative was planned to provide practical assistance to children in households where parents work late into the night or struggle to prepare meals on time due to health issues. This support goes beyond simple material distribution, carrying deeper significance as it was conducted in partnership with the social welfare corporation Uyang Foundation. It is widely evaluated as a great help to neighbors amid the rising cost of living ahead of the holiday.

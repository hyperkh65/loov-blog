Early personal computer keyboards featured a variety of function key symbols, but most were replaced with text labels as office users rejected confusing icons. Historically, the United States preferred text labels, while Europe favored icons; in multilingual Europe, a common visual language was highly useful for cross-border travel and product sales. In the evolution toward today's personal computers and Windows keyboards, only four types of symbols became widely established: Shift, Enter, Tab, and Backspace. Among the symbols that have since faded into memory are Print Screen, which depicted the screen turning into paper, as well as key click sounds and Home.

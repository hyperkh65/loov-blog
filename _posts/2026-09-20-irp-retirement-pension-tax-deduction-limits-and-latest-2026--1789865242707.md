The IRP (Individual Retirement Pension) account is an essential financial product for employees, offering tax deductions on annual contributions of up to 9 million KRW. For employees in their 40s and 50s preparing for retirement, it is the most reliable way to secure both tax benefits and retirement funds. While it was once viewed merely as a place to store severance pay, it has evolved into a versatile account capable of holding a wide variety of investment products. Notably, starting this year, you can directly purchase 10-year and 20-year government bonds through Defined Contribution (DC) retirement pensions and IRPs. In this article, we will explore specific strategies for saving on taxes and growing your retirement assets using an IRP account.

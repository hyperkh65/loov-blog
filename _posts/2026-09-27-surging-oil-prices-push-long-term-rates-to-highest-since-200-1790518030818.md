Nasdaq futures are showing strong downward pressure, falling over 1.1% even before the opening bell, while S&P 500 futures are down 0.6%. This simultaneous weakness is not merely a temporary fluctuation but a signal reflecting the structural trend of prolonged interest rate tightening.

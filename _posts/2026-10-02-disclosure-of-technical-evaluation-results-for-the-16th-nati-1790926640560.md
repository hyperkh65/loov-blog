The technical evaluation results for the service proposal of the 16th National Recommendation Awards Ceremony were announced today. This disclosure is a measure aimed at enhancing the transparency of the award process and helping the public understand it better. The Ministry of the Interior and Safety has published the scores for each evaluation criterion along with the opinions of the review committee. This marks the first time that such detailed information, previously kept confidential, has been made public. Consequently, interested citizens and relevant organizations can verify the objectivity of the evaluation through this material. This article will examine the key disclosed contents and the changes expected based on them.

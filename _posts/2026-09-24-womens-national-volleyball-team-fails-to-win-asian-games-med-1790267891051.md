The South Korean women's national volleyball team suffered a painful setback, failing to secure a medal for the second consecutive time after losing to Thailand 0-3 in the bronze medal match at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games. Representing South Korea, ranked 28th in the world and led by head coach Cha Sang-hyun, the team gave their all in the match held at Park Arena Komaki in Aichi Prefecture, Japan, but ultimately faced a disappointing result. Failing to win a medal at this tournament is only the third time in history since the 1962 Jakarta Games, making the performance shockingly poor. In particular, questionable calls by the head referee, which disrupted the team's early momentum, proved to be the decisive variable in determining the outcome.

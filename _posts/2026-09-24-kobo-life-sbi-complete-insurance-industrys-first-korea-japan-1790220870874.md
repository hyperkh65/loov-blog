Kobo Life, in collaboration with Japan's SBI Group, has successfully verified an innovative structure that allows institutional funds to be transferred directly between the Japanese Yen and the South Korean Won without going through the US Dollar. This achievement is recorded as the first case in the domestic insurance industry to verify the entire process of cross-border institutional fund transfer, exchange, and settlement. It lays the technical foundation to drastically reduce the complex intermediary steps typically involved in international remittances and currency exchanges. Unlike the double currency conversion fees we often lose when making direct overseas purchases, it is now possible for financial institutions to convert Won directly into Yen in a single step.

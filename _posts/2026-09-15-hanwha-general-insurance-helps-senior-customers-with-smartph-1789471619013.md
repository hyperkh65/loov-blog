Hanwha General Insurance has taken proactive steps to support its elderly customers, who face significant challenges in the rapidly evolving non-face-to-face financial environment, earning widespread acclaim. This initiative was born from the observation that 78.7% of visitors to its customer service centers are aged 60 or older, prompting the company to provide practical assistance. The program goes far beyond simple knowledge transfer, featuring a hands-on approach where participants directly interact with smartphones to learn. Seniors from the Gyeonggi region, including Incheon, Bucheon, and Bupyeong, gathered at the headquarters training center to learn new technologies with bright smiles.

There are cases where, despite diligent contributions, you remain in a blind spot regarding tax benefits. This is often due to the habit of failing to meet the annual contribution limit of 6 million KRW for pension savings funds and postponing it to the following year. Considering that the preparation period for year-end tax settlement is already underway through the first half of 2026, now is the most critical time. It is not enough to simply open an account and deposit money. You need the skill to withstand yield volatility while maximizing tax benefits. In this article, we explain why asset allocation is important right now through real-world examples. We will also look at the differences between pension savings funds and individual pension accounts (IRP).

KDB Life successfully held the 4th "Earth Do" Festival at Gwanghwamun Square in the heart of Seoul, sharing the importance of the environment with citizens. This event was designed to allow anyone to experience various climate actions that are easy to practice in daily life, rather than focusing on grand theories, and it received a warm response. On the weekend, countless citizens who visited Gwanghwamun Square participated in diverse environmental programs, enjoying a precious time to reflect on the meaning of environmental protection. This festival, organized by KDB Life in partnership with the Korea Environmental Corporation, holds significant meaning as it demonstrates a company taking the lead in expanding eco-friendly culture.

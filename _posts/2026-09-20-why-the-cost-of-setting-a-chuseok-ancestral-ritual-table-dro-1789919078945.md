The average cost of setting a Chuseok ancestral ritual table this year has been calculated at 196,630 won, a 1.5% decrease compared to the same period last year. Despite widespread concerns that grocery prices would surge with the holiday just around the corner, prices for certain seasonal items have fortunately stabilized. According to a survey conducted by the Korea Agro-Fisheries & Food Trade Corporation (aT) targeting traditional markets and large retailers nationwide, increased supply of fruits and seafood drove the overall cost down.

A new system requiring companies to disclose the government-announced land price in the notes to their financial statements will be fully implemented, allowing investors to easily assess the actual value of land assets held by corporations. The Financial Services Commission (FSC) recently held a meeting and finalized the amendment to the Korean Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises (K-IFRS) containing this provision. Previously, companies recorded land assets on their financial statements using the cost method, making it difficult to determine the difference between the book value and the actual market price.

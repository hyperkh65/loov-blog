Shinhan Bank has successfully secured the operating rights for Incheon Metropolitan City's primary treasury account, firmly maintaining its position as the traditional custodian of the city's funds for 24 years despite fierce competition. The city recently held a Treasury Designation Review Committee meeting and announced that Shinhan Bank was ultimately selected as the primary operator for the general account and the public enterprise special account. Following this selection, Shinhan Bank will take on the significant responsibility of stably managing the city's substantial finances for approximately five years, from early next year through the end of 2030.

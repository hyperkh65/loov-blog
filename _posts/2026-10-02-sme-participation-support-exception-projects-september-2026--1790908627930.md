On September 29, 2026, the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of the Ministry of Science and ICT officially announced the review results for the SME Participation Support Exception Projects. This announcement is based on Article 48 of the Software Promotion Act and serves as a regulatory mechanism to support the participation of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in public software projects, provided they meet certain conditions. The initiative stems from the reality that many SME representatives hesitate to enter new markets due to excessive financial statement audits and performance verification procedures demanded by major shareholders.

In early 2024, sales surged after Jang Won-young, a member of the girl group IVE, recommended the book on Instagram. Subsequently, it maintained a top spot on bestseller lists at online bookstores for several weeks, and a large-print edition was also released. Therefore, it is a suitable choice for busy modern individuals who want to approach Buddhist doctrines without feeling burdened.

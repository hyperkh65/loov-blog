The core of retirement pension withdrawal methods is not to take everything out as a lump sum, but to transfer the funds to an Individual Retirement Pension (IRP) account and receive them as a pension, which significantly reduces taxes. Recently, Manager Kim, who had just left a job he loved, was so happy about having a large sum of money that he received his severance pay directly into his regular checking account. He later deeply regretted this after paying several million won in taxes. If Manager Kim had chosen to receive his funds as a pension through an IRP account, he could have received a tax reduction of up to 30% on his retirement income tax, making his current situation even more regrettable.

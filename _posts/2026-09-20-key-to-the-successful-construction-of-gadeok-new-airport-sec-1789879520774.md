At a recent expert policy forum held at the Busan Chamber of Commerce, voices rose high calling for a response to the rapidly changing construction environment, especially as the basic design documents for the land reclamation works have been submitted and the start of priority construction is imminent within the year. The concern is that if the soaring raw material prices and international geopolitical instability are not reflected, serious disruptions could occur during the construction process.

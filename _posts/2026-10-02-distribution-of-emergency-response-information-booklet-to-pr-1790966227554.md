If a fire breaks out in a logistics warehouse storing hazardous chemicals, the firefighting water can mix with these substances and flow into rivers, potentially causing catastrophic secondary environmental pollution. To prevent this, the Chemical Safety Agency of the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment has officially announced the publication of the "Emergency Response Information Booklet for Hazardous Chemical Storage and Warehousing Businesses," which will be distributed to major cities, counties, and districts nationwide starting in September. This booklet is not just a map; it is a practical manual that clearly outlines how to contain water flow and identifies dangerous substances in the event of a fire.

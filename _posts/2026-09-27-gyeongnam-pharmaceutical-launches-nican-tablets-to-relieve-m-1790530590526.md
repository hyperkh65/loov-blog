title: "Gyeongnam Pharmaceutical Launches 'Nican Tablets' to Relieve Mouth Ulcers and Boost Vitamin B6"
date: 2026-09-27 17:36:30 +0900
---

Gyeongnam Pharmaceutical has introduced a new over-the-counter medication, "Nican Tablets," designed to address both Vitamin B6 supplementation and the relief of various mucosal disorders, including mouth ulcers. This is highly welcome news for modern individuals who often suffer from mouth ulcers, characterized by sores, stinging, and burning sensations, due to accumulated fatigue. In particular, it is expected to be a reliable health aid for women during pregnancy and breastfeeding, when immunity tends to drop significantly, as well as for patients who urgently need to recover their physical strength after illness. This product is receiving particular attention because it is not limited to the general consumer pharmacy market but is also actively targeting hospital prescription channels....

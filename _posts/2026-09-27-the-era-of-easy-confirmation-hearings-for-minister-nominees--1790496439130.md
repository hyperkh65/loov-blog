The unwritten rule in Yeouido that members of the National Assembly would smoothly pass confirmation hearings has now become a thing of the past. In fact, of the five cabinet nominees who failed to secure their posts since the inauguration of the Lee Jae-myung administration, a staggering four were former or current members of the National Assembly. The reason for this string of failures among ministerial nominees with political backgrounds lies in the significant gap between public expectations and the reality of the political landscape.

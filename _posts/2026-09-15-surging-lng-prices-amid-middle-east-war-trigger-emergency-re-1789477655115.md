As geopolitical conflicts in the Middle East drag on, liquefied natural gas (LNG) prices have skyrocketed to astronomical levels, dealing a direct blow to South Korea's power generation structure. The ambitious 2040 decarbonization roadmap initially announced by the government is not being upheld in practice; instead, a bizarre phenomenon is unfolding where the plan is effectively moving in reverse. The stated direction toward clean energy has vanished, replaced by a contradictory situation where power plants are increasing coal usage due to the inability to bear soaring fuel costs. This critical energy supply instability, which directly impacts the electricity bills we pay every day, has become a massive storm shaking the entire national economy, going beyond mere environmental concerns. ...

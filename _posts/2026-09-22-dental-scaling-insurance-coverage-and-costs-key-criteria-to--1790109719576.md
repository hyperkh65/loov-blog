If you haven't had this treatment yet this year, you should visit a dental clinic before the year ends. Many people postpone scaling because they find the process of removing tartar at the dentist cumbersome or the cost burdensome. However, by utilizing national health insurance benefits, you can maintain your dental health for a reasonable fee of around 10,000 KRW. It is crucial to remember that this benefit applies strictly within the period from January 1 to December 31 of each year, and any unused visits cannot be carried over to the following year.

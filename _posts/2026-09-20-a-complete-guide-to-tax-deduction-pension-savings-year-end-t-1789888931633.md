"Income deduction pension savings" is not an official legal term; rather, it is a colloquial expression referring to personal pension accounts opened to receive tax credits during year-end tax settlement. Every winter, it becomes a highly popular financial tool among office workers looking to boost their thirteenth-month salary. While many people still use the term "income deduction" based on past memories, the benefit is currently provided in the form of a tax credit. In this article, we will examine the exact deduction rates and limits for pension savings products and detail how to enroll to receive the maximum refund.

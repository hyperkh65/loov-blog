The cost of setting up the ancestral rites table for this year's Chuseok has decreased by an average of 1.5% compared to last year, coming in at 196,630 KRW, which alleviates the financial burden on household shopping. A survey conducted by the Korea Agro-Fisheries & Food Trade Corporation (aT) across traditional markets and large retail chains nationwide clearly confirmed this downward trend in prices. With the holiday break approaching, the government has taken the initiative to directly inspect the supply and demand status of major peak-season goods and the on-site response to discount campaigns.

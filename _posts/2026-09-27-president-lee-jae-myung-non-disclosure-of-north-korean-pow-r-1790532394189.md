President Lee Jae-myung directly explained that the government's decision to keep the repatriation of two North Korean prisoners of war (POWs) captured by Ukrainian forces non-public was a valid decision, considering national security and the safety of the individuals involved, even if it might help restore the administration's approval rating. Currently on a state visit to Mexico, the President stated this via his social media channels, expressing regret that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy unilaterally disclosed the matter during his UN General Assembly speech. This incident has emerged as a representative case illustrating how grave the government's decision-making process must be when handling diplomatic sensitivity and national security.

On the first day of the Korea Exchange (KRX) officially entering the after-hours stock trading market, known as the after-market, it was confirmed that the trading scale of NextTrade, which had previously monopolized this sector, has noticeably decreased. The KRX after-market, which operated from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM on the 14th, recorded a trading value of over 1.8 trillion won, marking a successful first step. However, this has led to a diversion of investors who previously traded stocks during nighttime hours, resulting in a red flag on NextTrade's scorecard. Attention is now focused on how the newly launched KRX nighttime market will shake the position of the existing alternative trading system (ATS).

The government has completely restructured the Innovative Pharmaceutical Enterprise (IPE) system by dividing it into "Leader" and "Leap" categories. This reform aims to improve the fundamental structure of the domestic pharmaceutical and biotechnology industry and enable promising startups to leap to world-class levels. The core objective of this overhaul is to overcome the limitations of the past, where companies of varying sizes and capabilities were evaluated by the same standards, and to provide precise support tailored to each company's growth stage.

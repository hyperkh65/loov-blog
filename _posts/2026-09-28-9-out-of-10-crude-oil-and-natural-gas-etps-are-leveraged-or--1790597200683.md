Among exchange-traded products (ETPs) related to crude oil and natural gas, a staggering nine out of ten are structured as leveraged or inverse products, necessitating extreme caution from investors. According to recent market trend reports released by financial regulators, while the overall size of the commodity ETP market has increased slightly, the concentration in high-risk products has reached a critical level. In particular, over 93% of natural gas products and more than 88% of crude oil products adopt high-risk derivative structures.

Employment insurance unemployment benefits are a public system designed to support the livelihood of workers who have been involuntarily separated from their jobs while they search for new employment. To qualify for these benefits, you must have been enrolled in employment insurance for at least 180 days within the 18 months prior to your separation. Understanding the application process and eligibility criteria can help alleviate financial burdens even after you have begun your job search. In particular, unemployment benefits serve not only as financial support but also play a role in mitigating the mental stress caused by job loss during the job-hunting process.

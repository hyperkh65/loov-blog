At the request of the developer, the schedule for the institutional recommendation for the special allocation for persons with disabilities at Bucheon Daerang How Story Descent has been changed to the first phase. The period for in-person applications at the community center remains the same as before, but the detailed schedule, including the resident recruitment announcement and the application for score adjustment, has been postponed. Eligible applicants should check the revised dates to ensure they are prepared to submit their documents without any issues.

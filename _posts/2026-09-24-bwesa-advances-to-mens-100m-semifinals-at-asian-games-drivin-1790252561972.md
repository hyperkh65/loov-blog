Bwesa Daniel Gasama, known as one of the twin engines of Korean short-distance track and field, successfully advanced to the semifinals of the men's 100m at the 2026 Asian Games, finishing second in his heat with an impressive time of 10.23 seconds. Representing the Ansan City Hall team, Bwesa displayed explosive acceleration during the race, drawing awe from spectators on-site. He also matched the exact time of his fellow competitor, Namadi Joel Jin, showcasing perfect synchronization. Many people are moved by seeing such world-class performances in Korean short-distance track and field. The two athletes are currently on a fierce pursuit of Kim Gu-yeong's national record of 10.07 seconds, which has stood unbroken for nine years.

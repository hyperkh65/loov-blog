A parking account is a deposit product that allows free deposits and withdrawals, earning interest even if funds are held for just one day. It is the most suitable financial product for managing short-term funds that you don't need immediately but don't want to lock away either. With recent increases in the base interest rate, commercial banks and savings banks are aggressively competing to offer various high-interest parking account products, meaning you can secure much more favorable terms with a little effort.

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT) stated that additional safety verification is indispensable following the discovery of construction errors, such as missing rebar, in the GTX-A Samseong Station section. Simultaneously, the ministry is reviewing a plan to claim damages from Seoul City for financial losses incurred due to construction delays. Seoul City has argued that the ongoing detailed safety diagnosis has rated the structural safety as Grade A, urging a prompt decision on the reinforcement plan. However, MOLIT maintains that the Grade A rating is an evaluation of the current construction stage only, and comprehensive verification considering post-completion train operations and disaster scenarios is necessary.

Seoul apartment transaction prices have recorded an upward trend for 85 consecutive weeks, tying the longest rising streak in history. According to recent weekly price trend data released by the Korea Real Estate Board, home prices across Seoul rose by an average of 0.13% compared to the previous week, indicating that the market's heat remains undiminished. Contrary to common perception, major apartment complexes in Gangnam are actually showing a weak trend with falling prices. However, large-scale complexes near subway stations in relatively affordable areas such as Seodaemun-gu and Dongdaemun-gu are firmly supporting the price increases.

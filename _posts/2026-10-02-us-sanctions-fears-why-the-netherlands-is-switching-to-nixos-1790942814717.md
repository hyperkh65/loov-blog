In response to anxieties over digital independence caused by US sanctions, the Dutch government is drastically reducing its reliance on Microsoft and building a new government work environment based on NixOS. This is not merely a technical replacement but a strategic decision to secure sovereignty over core digital infrastructure from a national security perspective. The direct trigger was the incident where the Chief Prosecutor of the International Criminal Court (ICC) was blocked from accessing essential work tools, such as email, due to US government sanctions.

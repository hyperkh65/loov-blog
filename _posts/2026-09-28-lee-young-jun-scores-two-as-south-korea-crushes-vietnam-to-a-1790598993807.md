The South Korea men's national football team secured a decisive 4-0 victory over Vietnam in the quarterfinals of the Asian Games men's football tournament, held on the 25th at the Mizuho Park Rugby Stadium in Nagoya, Japan. This win is highly significant as it thoroughly washes away the painful memories of the recent age-group tournament in January and firmly establishes the team's path toward a fourth consecutive Asian Games title. Manager Lee Min-sung's ambitious "aerial bombardment" tactic proved to be a perfect fit, allowing the team to play with refreshing ease. In particular, the tallest attacker, Lee Young-jun, scored a brace, playing a pivotal role as the spearhead of the attack.

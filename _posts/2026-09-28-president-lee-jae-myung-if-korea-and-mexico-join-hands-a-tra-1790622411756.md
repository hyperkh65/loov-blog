During his state visit to Mexico City, President Lee Jae-myung strongly stated that if Korea and Mexico join hands, it will be a prime opportunity to secure a leading position in the global market. He also expressed regret that despite the remarkable cooperation achievements between the two countries, they have not yet progressed to the stage of a comprehensive trade agreement. With the free trade agreement negotiations, which were suspended in 2006, still unresolved, the situation calls for a new breakthrough. Attention is focused on whether this visit will establish a robust institutional foundation that allows business people from both nations to operate in a more stable environment.

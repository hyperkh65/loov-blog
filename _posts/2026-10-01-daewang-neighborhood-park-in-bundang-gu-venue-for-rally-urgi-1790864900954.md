On the afternoon of October 1, 2026, Daewang Neighborhood Park in Bundang-gu became a hub for citizens' voices demanding the construction of the Gyeonggi Southern Metropolitan Railway. Seongnam Mayor Shin Sang-jin and Yongsin Special City Mayor Lee Sang-il, along with representatives from the Citizens' Alliance, earnestly requested that the railway be included in the 5th National Railway Network Plan. For residents of Bundang, this gathering is not just a park event; it is recorded as a pivotal moment foreshadowing a solution to the transportation challenges in the southern part of the metropolitan area. We will examine the facts of the day, their significance, and the changes that lie ahead from a realistic perspective.

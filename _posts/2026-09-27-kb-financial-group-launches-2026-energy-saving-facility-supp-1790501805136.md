KB Financial Group is partnering with the Foundation for Cooperation between Large and SMEs and the Fisheries and Agriculture Sector to fully launch the "2026 KB Financial Energy-Saving Facility Support Program." This initiative aims to support carbon neutrality and ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) practices among local small business owners in 2026. With a total investment of 600 million won, the program is designed to provide substantial improvements to business environments, offering relief to self-employed individuals in local commercial districts who have been struggling with high fixed costs such as electricity bills.

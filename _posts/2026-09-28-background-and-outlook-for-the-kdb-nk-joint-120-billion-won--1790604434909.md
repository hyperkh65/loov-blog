This recently announced financial support initiative is expected to serve as a solid foundation, helping small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and venture companies that struggle with fundraising to settle stably in the capital markets. By joining forces, the state-owned banks are taking on the role of a catalyst to inject vitality into the private investment market.

It begins with utilizing the heat and gas discarded from sewage treatment plants to gift a warm winter to nearby villages. The Ministry of Environment, together with Sejong Special Self-Governing City and Korea District Heating Corporation, signed a memorandum of understanding on September 28 to actively utilize the sewage heat and biogas generated at the Sejong Water Quality Restoration Center. This agreement stems from the goal of realizing energy self-sufficiency by recognizing that sewage treatment facilities operate 24/7 and consume significant energy, and by recycling that energy for district heating.

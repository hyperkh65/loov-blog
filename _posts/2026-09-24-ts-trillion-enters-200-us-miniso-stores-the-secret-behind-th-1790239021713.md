TS Trillion has achieved a significant milestone by partnering with Cheongdam Global to launch GD Shampoo, a haircare product exclusively for teenagers, in 200 Miniso stores across the United States. This expansion covers half of the 400 Miniso locations in the US, demonstrating the brand's rapidly growing influence in the local market. The move is particularly noteworthy as it extends the brand's reach to offline retail, building on its strong performance across various sales channels, including Amazon. Parents who have long struggled with issues such as scalp odor or excess oil in their children's hair will likely welcome this North American expansion with great enthusiasm.

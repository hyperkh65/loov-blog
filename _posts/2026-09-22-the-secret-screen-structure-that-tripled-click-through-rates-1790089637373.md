Making necessary suggestions stand out naturally without interfering with users' core tasks has long been a persistent challenge for all product developers. On the Toss Bank interface, the asset management report feature was originally positioned as a banner at the bottom of the transaction area, but its click-through rate (CTR) remained a mere 2%. This project began with the question: why don't people click on such a useful recommendation that perfectly fits their situation? We hypothesized that in a complex environment overflowing with information, the feature was being overlooked simply because it failed to distinguish itself from surrounding elements.

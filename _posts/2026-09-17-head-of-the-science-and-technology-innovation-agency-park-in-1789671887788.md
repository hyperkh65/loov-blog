Park In-gyu, Head of the Science and Technology Innovation Agency (STIA), is actively soliciting diverse opinions from the industry to formulate the FY2027 government research and development (R&D) budget. This initiative aims to incorporate the voices of businesses as part of the effort to determine how national budgets should be utilized effectively in actual technology fields. While budget allocation in the past often relied on theoretical approaches, there is now a notable effort to directly reflect the challenges faced by practitioners in the field. For instance, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and companies in emerging industries often face difficulties due to the gap between the nature of the research funding they immediately need and the support provided by the government...

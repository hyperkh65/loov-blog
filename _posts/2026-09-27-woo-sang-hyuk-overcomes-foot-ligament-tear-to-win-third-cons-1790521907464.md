Woo Sang-hyuk, the star of Korean high jump, won the bronze medal in the men's high jump final at the Mizho Park Athletics Stadium in Nagoya, Japan, on September 27, 2026, by clearing 2.22 meters. This event brought great joy to our delegation on the last day of the Chuseok holiday while also revealing the hidden pain he endured. While many naturally expected a gold medal, leaving some disappointment, his fighting spirit provided a moving experience that went beyond the color of the medal.

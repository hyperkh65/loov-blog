Shinhan Bank has successfully secured the operating rights for Incheon Metropolitan City's No. 1 Treasury again, firmly maintaining its position as the traditional treasury manager for 24 years despite fierce competition. Incheon City recently held a Treasury Designation Review Committee and announced that Shinhan Bank was finally selected as the main operator for the No. 1 Treasury, which manages the general account and the special account for public enterprises. With this selection, Shinhan Bank has been entrusted with the heavy responsibility of stably managing Incheon City's massive finances for approximately five years, from early next year until the end of 2030.

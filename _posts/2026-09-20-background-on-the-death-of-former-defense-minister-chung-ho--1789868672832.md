Former Defense Minister Chung Ho-yong, who was a key power broker in the New Military Faction during the December 12 military coup and the May 18 Democratic Uprising, recently passed away at the age of 94. A classmate of former presidents Chun Doo-hwan and Roh Tae-woo from the 11th class of the Korea Military Academy, Chung played a pivotal role in the military clique known as the "Hana-hoe" (One Association), laying the groundwork for the regime's establishment. His life, which ended after a period of declining health and hospital treatment, starkly reveals the darkest aspects of modern Korean history.

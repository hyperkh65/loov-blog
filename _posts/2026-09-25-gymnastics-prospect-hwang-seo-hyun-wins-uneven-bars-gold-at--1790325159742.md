Hwang Seo-hyun, a 17-year-old gymnastics prospect currently in her second year at Incheon Sports High School, proudly claimed the gold medal in the women's uneven bars final at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games. This victory marks a precious achievement, being the first gold medal for Korean women's artistic gymnastics in 40 years, and deeply moved many citizens. The junior athlete's bold determination, expressed in a pre-departure interview where she simply wished to perform without regret, has now become reality. Hwang's performance was truly astonishing, showcasing a flawless and unwavering routine despite her small stature.

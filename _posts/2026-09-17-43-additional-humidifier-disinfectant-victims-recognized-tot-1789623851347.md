As 43 new victims of humidifier disinfectants were officially recognized by the government, the cumulative number of individuals eligible for relief benefits has increased to 6,080. The Ministry of Climate, Energy and the Environment recently convened the 51st Victim Relief Committee in a meeting room near Seoul Station, where it meticulously reviewed a total of 176 cases. As a result, the committee finalized the payment of relief benefits and the determination of damage grades for 115 individuals, offering a glimmer of hope to those who have suffered for a long time.

Woo Sang-hyuk, South Korea's proud high jump star, won a bronze medal in the men's high jump final at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games with a mark of 2.22m, setting a remarkable record of winning a medal at three consecutive Games. Following his silver medals at the 2018 Jakarta-Palembang and 2023 Hangzhou Games, he added another valuable medal in Nagoya, once again proving his top-tier status on the Asian stage. This achievement is particularly moving because it was accomplished despite heavy rain and the lingering effects of injury. Even after failing his final jump, his bright smile and applause toward the crowd demonstrated true sportsmanship.

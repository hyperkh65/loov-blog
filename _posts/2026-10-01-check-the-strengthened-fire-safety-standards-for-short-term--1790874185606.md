Starting in October, fire and safety standards will be mandatorily applied to short-term rental accommodations like Airbnb. Regulations have been strengthened to prohibit operations if smoke detectors, carbon monoxide alarms, and fire extinguishers are not installed. This aligns with the global trend of tightening regulations on the accommodation industry in Europe and major cities, making the safety of domestic and international users a key issue. When booking accommodations for travel or business trips, you should carefully verify that safety facilities are properly in place.

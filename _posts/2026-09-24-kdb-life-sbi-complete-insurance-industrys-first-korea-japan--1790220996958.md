KDB Life, in collaboration with Japan's SBI Group, has successfully validated an innovative structure that allows institutional funds to be transferred directly between the Japanese Yen and the South Korean Won without going through the US Dollar. This achievement is recorded as the first case in the domestic insurance industry to validate the entire process of cross-border institutional fund transfer, exchange, and settlement. It lays the technical foundation to drastically reduce the complex intermediary steps typically involved in international remittances and currency exchanges. Unlike the double currency conversion fees often lost during overseas direct purchases, this technology enables a "magical" direct conversion from Won to Yen between financial institutions.

The finals of the 3rd Show Me the Acting competition, where 20 rising actors compete fiercely solely on the basis of a single 90-second monologue performance, grandly opened on September 19 at the Central University Seoul Campus Small Theater in Heukseok-dong, Dongjak-gu, Seoul. This event, which proves potential purely through short-form delivery and dialogue handling without a lengthy audition process, has firmly established itself as a breeding ground for new talent, marking its third year. Given the intense interest from hundreds of applicants every year, this final stage continued to yield significant results in discovering the next generation of leading actors.

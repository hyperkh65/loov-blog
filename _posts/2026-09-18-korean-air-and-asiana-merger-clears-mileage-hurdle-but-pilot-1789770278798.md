Korean Air has received final approval from the Fair Trade Commission (FTC) for the most challenging aspect of its integration with Asiana Airlines: the mileage consolidation plan. This marks the successful overcoming of the biggest hurdle in the merger process. It took a full 15 months from the initial submission of the draft last year, during which the plan underwent multiple revisions to address consumer concerns. Now, with the final merger date set for December 17, two urgent issues remain: integrating the promotion seniority of pilots from both airlines and restructuring shares in subsidiaries.

The Housing and Urban Guarantee Corporation (HUG) has announced that it will recruit residents for its 12th round of Steady Jeonse Housing, offering a total of 900 units in urban areas of the Seoul metropolitan region, including Seoul, Incheon, and Bucheon in Gyeonggi Province, as well as Busan. This recruitment marks the largest scale in the history of HUG's announcements, and any member of a household without home ownership is eligible to apply. There is no worry about not receiving the jeonse (deposit) back, and residents can live at approximately 90% of the surrounding market rate, making it highly popular.

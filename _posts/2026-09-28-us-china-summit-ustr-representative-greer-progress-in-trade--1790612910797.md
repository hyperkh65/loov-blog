USTR Representative Greer announced that progress has been made in trade negotiations with China on the occasion of the US-China summit. He stated that he plans to announce the detailed results of the negotiations on Monday. This progress is being concretized through measures such as expanded agricultural purchases and commitments to rare earth supply. Additionally, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng agreed to extend the existing trade truce by two more months. This is assessed as providing market stability for both countries.

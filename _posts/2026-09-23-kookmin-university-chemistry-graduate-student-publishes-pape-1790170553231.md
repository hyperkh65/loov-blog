title: "Kookmin University Chemistry Graduate Student Publishes Paper on Synthetic Resin Recycling via Ultra-Fast Infrared Video Analysis Technology"
date: 2026-09-23 13:35:53 +0900
---

A graduate student in the Department of Chemistry at Kookmin University has developed ultra-fast infrared video analysis technology, proposing a method to enhance the efficiency of synthetic resin recycling. This research significantly improves the processing speed of resource circulation processes by enabling rapid analysis and sorting of waste synthetic resins. The core of this achievement is the development of ultra-fast band-selective infrared imaging technology, which measures only six key short-wave infrared wavelengths. Based on chemical fingerprints, this technology can measure multiple waste synthetic resin flake materials within 2 seconds and distinguish between 7 types of synthetic resins and foreign objects with high accuracy using deep learning object recognition.

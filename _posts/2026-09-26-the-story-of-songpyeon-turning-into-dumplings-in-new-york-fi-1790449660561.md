First Lady Kim Hye-kyung, who visited New York, USA, to attend the UN General Assembly, enjoyed a warm holiday atmosphere in advance by making songpyeon (rice cakes) with local Korean compatriots. At the event held on the 23rd (local time) at the Korean Cultural Center in New York, the First Lady shared a joke about her songpyeon not taking the right shape, bringing a great deal of laughter. She reminisced about childhood memories of preparing holiday foods with relatives and shared her thoughts on Korean cuisine, which is enjoying immense popularity on the global stage. This gathering served as a precious time to comfort the hearts of compatriots celebrating the holiday abroad and to feel the changed status of Korean culture.

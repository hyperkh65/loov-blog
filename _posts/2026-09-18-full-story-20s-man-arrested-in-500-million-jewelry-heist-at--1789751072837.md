A man in his 20s who stole jewelry worth a staggering 500 million won from a gold and jewelry store inside a large hypermarket in Incheon has been arrested in Seoul on the same day the crime occurred, thanks to the police's swift pursuit. The incident, which took place in the early hours of September 14, 2026, exploited security loopholes in the hypermarket building after business hours. The theft involved a massive 173 pieces of jewelry, signaling a significant loss. Fortunately, the police acted quickly to recover all the stolen items and are currently investigating whether there were accomplices and the exact modus operandi.

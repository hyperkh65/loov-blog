A fascinating survey revealed that medical expenses paid by card at hospital counters have increased by over 16% over the past two years. Scrutinizing card transaction records from local clinics to large general hospitals reveals the pain points and interests of our society. Visits to internal medicine departments remain overwhelmingly the most common, driven by everyday ailments like colds and mild stomachaches. However, behind this lies a bitter reality: the number of pediatric and adolescent medicine consultations has significantly declined, hit hard by the low birth rate. Conversely, as the trend of spending generously on appearance enhancement spreads, card payment amounts for plastic surgery have followed a steep upward curve.

The Kookmin University Anchor Project Team held a results sharing event for the "Video-Based AI Content Production Project" at the NC AI headquarters, unveiling video works created directly by students. This event was organized to share the outcomes of a non-curricular program that ran for approximately four months starting from last May. Participating students were able to significantly enhance their practical skills by working with the latest technologies actually used in the industry. It holds great significance because the program did not stop at simple theoretical education but allowed students to experience the entire process, from planning to large-screen screening.

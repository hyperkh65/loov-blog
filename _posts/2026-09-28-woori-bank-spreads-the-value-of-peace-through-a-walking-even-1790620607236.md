Woori Bank is taking meaningful steps to honor the legacy of Mr. Baekbeom Kim Gu and promote the values of peace and cultural heritage preservation. Held in the Yongsan district of Seoul on the occasion of World Peace Day, this event served as an opportunity to reflect on the preciousness of our culture by walking in the footsteps of a historical figure. The event carries even greater significance this year, as it marks the 150th anniversary of Mr. Kim Gu's birth, designated by UNESCO. Many citizens, who typically spend their weekends glued to their smartphones or visiting nearby cafes, were able to experience history firsthand by walking with their own feet.

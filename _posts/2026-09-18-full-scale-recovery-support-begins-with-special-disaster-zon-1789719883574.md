Local governments that suffered significant damage from the heavy rains between late August and early September have been designated as special disaster zones by the government, accelerating support for the restoration of daily life. This measure was established to provide practical assistance to residents who lost their livelihoods due to sudden torrential rains, including house flooding and the loss of farmland. The Ministry of the Interior and Safety promptly investigated the scale of the damage and finalized the target areas after deliberation by the Central Disaster Management Headquarters. Fortunately, this announcement has created the conditions for active national funding to be injected into sites where recovery was daunting using only local government budgets.

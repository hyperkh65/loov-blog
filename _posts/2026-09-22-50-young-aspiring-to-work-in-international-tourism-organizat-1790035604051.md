Fifty young people recently successfully completed the practical job training program for international tourism organizations, hosted by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Korea Tourism Organization, laying the groundwork for their entry into the global stage. Conducted over two days from the 18th to the 19th at the Seoul Center, this training served as a practical guide for young people eager to showcase their capabilities on the world stage. It was a great help for job seekers who had vaguely dreamed of working for global organizations, offering them fresh advice from industry experts and tailored mentoring programs. In this article, we will take a detailed look at the specific contents of this practical training and the prospects expected ahead.

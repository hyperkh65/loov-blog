Woo Sang-hyuk, the face of Korean high jump, set a great milestone by winning a bronze medal in the men's high jump final at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games with a mark of 2.22 meters, securing a medal for the third consecutive Games. Despite a long three-month hiatus due to the aftermath of an injury sustained in May, he demonstrated an indomitable fighting spirit, refusing to give up until the very end. On the day of the competition, harsh conditions persisted with heavy rain falling at the Mizho Park Athletics Stadium in Nagoya, Japan, yet he did not lose his characteristic bright smile. Although he did not reach the top spot to win the gold medal in his fourth attempt at the Asian Games, the weight of the process was by no means light.

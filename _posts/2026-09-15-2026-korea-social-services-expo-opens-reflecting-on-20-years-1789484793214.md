The 2026 Korea Social Services Expo, co-hosted by the Ministry of Health and Welfare and the Korea Social Services Corporation, is set to open its grand curtain at the aT Center Exhibition Hall 2 in Seocho-gu, Seoul. This event has been organized to look back on the 20-year journey since the introduction of the social services system and to share a vision for the future. Those who have experienced welfare benefits in their daily lives will nod in agreement at how significant the changes we feel have been. On-site, various exhibitions and programs await visitors, offering a comprehensive view of the innovations achieved over the years.

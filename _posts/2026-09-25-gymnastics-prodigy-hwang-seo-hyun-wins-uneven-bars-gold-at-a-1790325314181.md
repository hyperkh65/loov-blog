This victory is a heartwarming achievement, marking the first gold medal for a South Korean woman in the uneven bars event in a whopping 40 years, and has received enthusiastic applause from the entire nation. In a pre-departure interview, the youngest member of the team had simply vowed to perform without regret, and that promise ultimately became reality through a flawless golden routine.

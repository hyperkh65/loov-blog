While this meeting focused on highlighting the personal bond between the two leaders, many remain skeptical about whether it will lead to the resolution of substantive conflicts. During the pre-summit welcome ceremony, President Trump mentioned the great friendship built over a long period and emphasized common interests. In response, President Xi stated that the slogan of the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation and the slogan of America's resurgence can move forward together.

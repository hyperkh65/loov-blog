A major shift is sweeping through the over-the-counter (OTC) stock market as Kiwoom Securities and Naver Pay Unlisted have signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) to introduce a cross-broker trading system for unlisted stocks. For years, the biggest hurdle in trading unlisted stocks was the requirement that buyers and sellers must hold accounts at the same brokerage firm for a transaction to be executed. For instance, if an investor held an account at one brokerage but found a desirable listing under a user of another brokerage, they were forced to either abandon the trade or go through the hassle of opening a new account.

Woori Financial Group is launching a large-scale, long-term development program to support young athletes who refuse to give up on sports despite financial hardships. The group has committed to providing a total of 3 billion won in support through 2036. Recently, at a major music festival held at Seoul Land in Gwacheon, Woori Financial Chairman Lim Jong-ryong officially announced the launch of "Woori Dream Bridge," a new sports social contribution initiative, demonstrating a strong commitment to cultivating future national team representatives. This initiative goes beyond one-off sponsorship; it aims to systematically identify 60 promising athletes each year, serving as a practical pillar that allows them to focus solely on their training without environmental constraints.

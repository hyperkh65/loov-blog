The Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the International Energy Agency (IEA) in Seoul on the 17th to strengthen climate crisis response and energy security in the Asian region. This collaboration represents an innovative step toward overcoming two massive crises simultaneously: energy supply instability stemming from the Middle East and global climate disasters. In fact, as tensions in the Middle East escalate, Asian countries with high dependence on fossil fuels are exposed to the risk of supply chain disruptions that could erupt at any moment.

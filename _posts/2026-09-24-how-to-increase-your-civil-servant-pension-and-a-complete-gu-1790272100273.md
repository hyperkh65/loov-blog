Retiring while relying solely on your civil servant pension can lead to unexpected financial gaps and the burden of rising inflation. It is common to see acquaintances who worked diligently for over 30 years struggle to cover their children's education or medical expenses with their monthly pension alone, forcing them to seek new employment. This happens because there is a period of income gap between retirement and the legally mandated pension commencement age. In this article, we will carefully examine the exact structure of the civil servant pension, methods to maximize your benefits, and realistic asset management strategies for retirement. Thorough preparation is the most reliable key to a comfortable and secure life after retirement.

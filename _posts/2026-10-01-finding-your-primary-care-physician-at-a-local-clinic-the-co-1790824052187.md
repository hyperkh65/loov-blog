Starting September 29, 2026, the Community Primary Care Innovation Pilot Program will officially begin, allowing residents aged 50 and older to select a dedicated primary care physician at a local clinic in their area. This program provides comprehensive support ranging from disease treatment to prevention and health management. Designed in response to the rapid transition into a super-aged society and the increasing number of patients with chronic diseases, this policy goes beyond simply treating ailments, aiming to organically connect everyday health management with care services.

The de facto power in the Korean capital market is now concentrating in specialized asset management companies rather than securities firms. This importance was proven at the 'Korea Premium Week' event, which is a key initiative by the Financial Services Commission and the exchange, where investment strategy seminars led by securities firms and asset management companies were featured as core lineup items. In the past, the role of securities firms, centered on the issuance market, was emphasized. However, the current focus has shifted to the management stage: how to manage investors' funds to generate returns.

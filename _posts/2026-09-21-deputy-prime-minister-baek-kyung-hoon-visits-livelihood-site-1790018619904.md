Deputy Prime Minister Baek Kyung-hoon visited livelihood sites and social welfare facilities across the country to practice warm comfort and sharing in celebration of Chuseok, the great traditional holiday of the Korean people. This initiative was organized to look after neighbors who are easily overlooked during the holiday season and to encourage the hard work of workers on the front lines. It holds great significance as the government has taken the lead to ensure that warmth spreads evenly to every corner of our society. It was also a precious opportunity to closely listen to the live voices of merchants and residents struggling with rising prices and an economic downturn.

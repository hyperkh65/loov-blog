In Gyeonggi and Gyeongbuk provinces, you can receive support for meeting room rental costs of up to 3.5 million KRW per year for a maximum of two years. This policy helps small businesses and non-profit organizations reduce their financial burden and hold gatherings more actively. The support can only be used for spaces operated directly by local governments or those with which they have signed agreements. This article outlines the eligibility criteria, amounts and durations, application procedures, precautions, and real-world use cases.

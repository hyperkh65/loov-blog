First, keep in mind that more than half of insurance payment rejections stem from what might seem like simple document omissions or formatting errors. Most readers easily fall into the misconception that portal-based claim systems like 'Silson24' will automatically resolve all issues. However, we must face the reality that even though the electronic claim ratio reached 74% as of 2026, some insurance companies have declared a suspension of data reception, leading to an increase in paid services or limited processing. This is particularly critical for nursing care insurance, where proof of meeting the criteria specified in the policy terms and evidence of actual nursing care service usage are essential.

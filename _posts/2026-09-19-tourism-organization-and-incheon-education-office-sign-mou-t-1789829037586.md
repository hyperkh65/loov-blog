The Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) and the Incheon Metropolitan Office of Education have joined forces to significantly reduce the safety burden associated with field trips, a major headache for schools. Recently, a trend of avoiding outdoor activities and school trips has become prevalent among local schools due to concerns over various safety accidents and administrative liability. Teachers felt fear of potential accidents that could occur while supervising students, and parents openly expressed their anxiety. However, the memorandum of understanding (MOU) signed by the two institutions contains specific and practical measures to alleviate this pressure on the educational front.

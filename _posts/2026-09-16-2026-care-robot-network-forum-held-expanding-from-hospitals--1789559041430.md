As we enter the super-aged society, caring for patients and the elderly has emerged as the most urgent challenge for our society. The event recently held is expected to provide answers to this pressing issue. Held by the National Rehabilitation Service, an agency under the Ministry of Health and Welfare, at the Seoul Baekbeom Kim Gu Memorial Hall, the event focused on expanding technologies that have been confined to hospital settings into daily life and homes. In particular, solutions that can greatly assist families caring for parents with mobility issues when professional caregivers are insufficient were discussed in depth.

The value of Hanwoo as a key driver for the future of sustainable agriculture has once again left a deep impression on the public. With the strong support of the Hanwoo Self-Help Fund, the National Hanwoo Association participated in the Korea Agriculture Expo held from September 10 to 13, widely promoting the excellence of Hanwoo as a healthy food source. This event was a meaningful occasion to share the history and importance of the Hanwoo industry, which has grown in harmony with our agriculture over many years, going beyond a simple food introduction. In particular, by transparently showcasing the entire process from production to consumption, the event approachably explained the role and value of Hanwoo in environmentally conscious agriculture to the general public.

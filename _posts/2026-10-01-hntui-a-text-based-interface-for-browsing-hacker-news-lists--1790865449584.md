If you need a tool to view Hacker News directly from your terminal, give Hntui a try. You can switch between the Latest, New, Best, Ask, Show, and Jobs lists like tabs and navigate quickly using Vim-style keybindings. Most actions are also supported via mouse, making it convenient and easy on the wrists. If you come across a post you like while reading, you can add it to your saved list with a single shortcut key and revisit it later. The saved list is stored in a local JSON file, so it persists even after sessions change. It offers both dark and light themes, allowing you to adjust the screen to your ambient lighting, and features a scanner effect during loading that catches the eye.

Former Governor Kim Dong-yeon has directly refuted current Governor Choo Mi-ae's criticism regarding the province's deteriorating finances, stating, "This is not a fiscal crisis," causing significant commotion in local politics. This marks the first time in over 100 days that the former governor has broken his silence in response to Governor Choo's strong criticism that the previous administration's expansionary fiscal policies led to the current financial difficulties. His core argument is that while operating a country or local government inevitably involves various challenges in budget management, immediately defining these issues as a catastrophic crisis is an exaggeration.

Woori Financial Group has decided to invest a total of 3 billion won over the next three years, in partnership with the Ministry of Health and Welfare and the Korea Community Chest, to support the social integration of youth preparing for independence and isolated or reclusive youth. This is very welcome news, as it aims to help young people who must step into the world alone without nearby support, or those who have been confined to their rooms for a long time, to establish themselves confidently as members of society. In the past, support often amounted to one-time financial aid, leaving many young people without substantive help and leading to repeated setbacks.
